# Governance

## Maintainers

amesml has a single maintainer, [Muhammad Ahsan](https://github.com/Ahsan-muhd-444),
who reviews and merges changes, sets the roadmap and publishes releases.
Ownership is recorded in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS).

## Decisions

Most decisions happen in issues and pull requests. Significant design choices
are recorded with their reasoning in [docs/DECISIONS.md](docs/DECISIONS.md), so
they can be revisited when circumstances change. Anyone may propose a change to
any of them by opening an issue.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have made several substantial, high-quality contributions -
code, reviews, documentation or triage - over at least three months may be
invited to become maintainers. You can also ask: open an issue describing your
contributions. New maintainers receive write access, are added to
`CODEOWNERS`, and follow the same review process as everyone else.

## Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part is expected to follow the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), which the maintainer enforces.

## Changing this document

Governance changes are made by pull request and stay open for at least seven
days so that contributors can comment.
